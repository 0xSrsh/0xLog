# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the 0xLog community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on improving the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, and publication of private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [0xSoroosh@gmail.com](mailto:0xSoroosh@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
